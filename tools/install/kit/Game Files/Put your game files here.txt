Put your own Black Ops II files in this folder, in any layout (subfolders are fine):

  - the game: your Xbox 360 disc image (.iso), or the folder extracted from it (the one with default_mp.xex)
  - Title Update 18 (required): the package tu00000002_00000000
  - your DLC packages, if you have them (optional)

Then double-click "Build bo2-360-nx.bat" in the kit's folder.
